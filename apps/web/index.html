Flood response, step by step

A dry run on the real December 2014 flood. The system detects danger and drafts the warning; nothing is sent until a person approves it.

Public demo · operator actions are restricted. Try the scripted walkthrough.

Landslide layer: reanalysis rain — levels likely under-estimated

  1. 1Replay the 2014 flood

    Feeds the real river and rain readings from December 2014 into the system, starting 18 Dec at about 30 seconds a day. When a river crosses its warning level, the system notices — and step 2 starts by itself.

  2. 2The agent drafts an alert

    Works out who is at risk, where they should go and how to get there, and writes the warning. Code checks every fact; each step shows in “Under the hood”.

    Don’t wait for a river to cross: assess the situation at the current replay time.
  3. 3Check and approve

    No alert yet. The draft appears here with its checks; you approve or reject it.
